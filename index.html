About the course

This course examines the foundational principles of designing computing systems that can withstand, adapt to, and recover from failure. Students will explore distributed systems, fault tolerance, redundancy, reliability engineering, consistency, disaster recovery, observability, and failure propagation.

Rather than focusing on specific cloud technologies, the course emphasizes enduring computer science and engineering concepts, using Amazon Web Services (AWS) as a practical environment for applying them. It also examines emerging resilience challenges in artificial intelligence and autonomous systems, including probabilistic behavior, cascading failures, and automated recovery.

Through hands-on exercises and architectural analysis, you will develop the skills to design and evaluate dependable systems across evolving computing environments.

  • Fault tolerance
  • Redundancy
  • Distributed systems
  • Consistency
  • Observability
  • Disaster recovery
  • Failure propagation
  • Chaos testing
  • Resilient AI systems

How a week works

Your team works as a consultancy for a fictional regional bank. Every Tuesday brings a new client problem.

  1. BuildDeploy a small piece of the bank’s system on AWS with Terraform.
  2. HardenAdd the week’s resilience pattern: health checks, retries, failover, alarms.
  3. BreakInject failures on purpose and measure what actually happens.
  4. RecommendPresent your fix to the client, with the cost and the risk.
